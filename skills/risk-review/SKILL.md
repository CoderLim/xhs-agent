# Risk Review Skill

## Purpose

Review a draft before publishing.

## Inputs

- title
- cover text
- draft
- claims
- offer

## Checks

1. Is the title aligned with the body?
2. Are the claims believable?
3. Are the conditions clear?
4. Is any personal story unsupported?
5. Is the draft too similar to a reference?
6. Is the next action clear and fair?

## Output

```text
Risk level:
Issues:
Suggested edits:
Safer title:
Safer cover text:
Final note:
```

## Rules

- Prefer precise wording.
- Avoid fixed result promises.
- Avoid unsupported metrics.
- Avoid copied stories or images.
