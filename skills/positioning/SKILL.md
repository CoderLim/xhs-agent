# Positioning Skill

## Purpose

Turn a vague account idea into a clear content direction.

## When to use

Use this skill when the user wants to start or refine an account.

## Inputs

- niche
- target reader
- creator background
- offer or business goal
- weekly capacity
- constraints

## Process

1. Identify the target reader.
2. Identify the key scene.
3. Identify the concrete problem.
4. Identify the desired outcome.
5. Match the creator background with reader demand.
6. Create content pillars.
7. Create a first 30-day direction.

## Output

```text
Positioning sentence:
Target reader:
Core scenes:
Core problems:
Content pillars:
Profile bio:
First 30-day direction:
Hypotheses to test:
```

## Quality rules

- Avoid broad labels.
- Prefer specific reader plus specific scene.
- Do not promise results.
- Keep business goal aligned with reader value.
