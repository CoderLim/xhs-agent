# Risk Control

The agent should optimize for trust and long-term reuse.

## Rules

1. Do not promise fixed results.
2. Do not treat one case as a universal rule.
3. Do not copy another person's story.
4. Do not copy another person's image.
5. Do not invent personal experience.
6. Do not invent metrics.
7. Do not use a title that conflicts with the body.
8. Do not hide important conditions.
9. Use softer wording when a claim is uncertain.
10. Mark every metric rule as a reference point.

## Review Checklist

Before final output, check:

- Is the reader specific?
- Is the scene specific?
- Is the problem specific?
- Is the outcome believable?
- Is the method actionable?
- Is any claim too strong?
- Is the next test clear?
